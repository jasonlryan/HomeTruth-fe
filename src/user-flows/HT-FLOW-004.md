# HT-FLOW-004 · Preparing to sell

**Version 0.1 · 18 November 2026 · Proposed for review · HT-349**

Help a seller identify gaps, explain improvements with evidence and choose what to share.

| Step | Customer action and result | Data and access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Start a checklist | See what sellers commonly gather. Mark what I have, what I need to find and what does not apply. Ask HomeTruth is available for general guidance. | Temporary checklist and stage. Request the minimum context needed for the answer. No private upload or account required here. |
| 2. Select the home | Choose an existing home or set one up after signing in. Resume the same checklist and preview which existing records could help. | [Property], [Property Person] and confirmed authority. A seller may also be buying a different home. Keep the contexts independent. |
| 3. Review the evidence | Connect work summaries to invoices, warranties or certificates in Vault. Flag missing records and keep a follow-up. | [Property Fact], [Property Document], [Evidence Source], [Reminder / Task]. Show provenance and distinguish source-backed claims from information still to confirm. |
| 4. Prepare the paperwork | Draft a private pack, choose the documents and inspect each item before sending. Open it again from the selected home. | Proposed sale-pack contract: property, selected evidence, review status and versions. Personal notes, unrelated documents and other properties remain outside the selection. |
| 5. Share by choice | Review the recipient, access and exact material before confirming. Show what was shared and whether access can be withdrawn. | Separate, purpose-specific grant with recipient, scope, expiry or revocation and audit record. Validate authority and access at the server. The control for sharing stays unavailable in the review prototype. |

Saving a checklist does not share data. With no supporting documents, still show a useful gaps list. A failed share leaves the private draft intact and must not show confirmation. Avoid promises about sale price or completeness of paperwork.

**Review check.** Start from an existing or empty record, identify one gap, choose evidence for a specific piece of work and inspect the exact proposed share. Review access and withdrawal before enabling a real send.
